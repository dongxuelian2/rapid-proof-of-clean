# Competitive positioning

Values are qualitative; performance varies by product, sampling plan, surface and
validation. `VARIES / SOURCE-DEPENDENT` is used where a universal number would be
misleading.

The proposed role is a rapid non-contact screening/verification layer, not a
replacement for every microbiological or chemical method. Laboratory or target-
specific methods remain escalation/compliance tools.

The safe differentiation is the combined workflow—multiple-reference confidence,
bounded inference, explicit UNKNOWN, controlled remeasurement and coverage log.
Structured light, modulation analysis, deflectometry, multiple patterns and optical
contamination imaging are not novelty claims.
