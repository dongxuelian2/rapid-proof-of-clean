# Claim ladder

Claims may move upward only when the stated evidence exists. Lower levels do not
imply higher ones.

## Level 1 — Proven mathematically / by implementation

- The repository implements four-phase demodulation, interval propagation,
  reference consensus, gain guard, two-state fusion and explicit abstention.
- Under the stated toy model and error bounds, the interval logic has the
  documented model-conditional behavior.
- These are software/mathematical facts, not physical-performance claims.

## Level 2 — Demonstrated in deterministic simulation

- The fixed synthetic v1 flags dirty-reference, uniform-absorber and negative-
  cancellation constructions while leaving the optically invisible construction
  proxy-PASSed.
- The reported 72-scene metrics and nuisance outcomes apply only to their fixed
  generators/configurations. Pixels are not independent trials.

## Level 3 — Literature-supported physical plausibility

- Structured-light modulation and deflectometry can reveal some contamination,
  surface and defect responses in published contexts.
- Reference calibration, multi-angle/wavelength inspection and cleaning-oriented
  optical comparisons have precedent.
- This supports a physical experiment, not transfer of published performance to
  this system.

## Level 4 — Proposed validation targets

- Repeatable hardware demodulation and physically measured noise/geometry bounds.
- A response for named safe residue surrogates on named surface materials.
- Locked coupon-level false-clean, false-flag, UNKNOWN and coverage intervals.
- Timed target-surface workflow within 30 minutes for a declared area/material set.
- Relevant-environment usability, reference lifecycle and field prototype cost.

## Level 5 — Forbidden claims at current evidence level

- real LOD, sensitivity, specificity or real-world false-clean rate;
- microbial/pathogen, species/strain, viability, ATP, sterility or disinfection;
- universal cleanliness, clinical/food-safety/regulatory compliance;
- whole-room single-pass coverage or measured under-30-minute performance;
- novelty, patentability, freedom to operate, commercial readiness or validated TRL 3+.

The final proposal stays at Levels 1–3 and presents Level 4 as future work. It
does not make Level 5 claims.
