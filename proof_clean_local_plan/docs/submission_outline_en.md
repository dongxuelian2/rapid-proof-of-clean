# Working outline for a participant-authored submission

This is an outline, not a ready-to-submit solution. Recheck the live form, field limits and agreement. Do not invent experience, validation, commercial readiness or intellectual-property conclusions. Complete the human research gates first.

## Participation, maturity and collaboration

State the actual participation category. Describe the maturity of the whole concept separately from its computational component. State whether collaboration is of interest. The participant makes all agreement and licensing decisions personally.

## Problem and opportunity

Describe one professional cleaning scenario and the exact surfaces and observable residues in scope. Explain why a negative measurement requires both valid reference information and adequate observability. Distinguish the proposed research contribution from existing structured-light modulation inspection.

## Solution overview

Working title: Reference-calibrated active reflectance screening with bounded-response decisions and coverage-aware measurement budgets.

Explain the proposed illumination, capture, reference, signal-quality checks, feasible response set and PASS/FLAG/UNKNOWN outputs. Restrict PASS to an experimentally calibrated residue-related optical criterion if and when that calibration exists. Do not imply microbial sterility. Clearly separate implemented computational modules from future sensing hardware and room mapping.

## Scientific basis and feasibility

Cite the closest published precedent. Explain that this prototype assumes a Gaussian reflection-transfer response and bounded measurement error. Describe the error propagation and interval rule from docs/mathematical_basis.md. State which physical assumptions have been experimentally checked by the participant, if any. A paper establishing a related optical mechanism does not validate this specific system.

## Performance and limitations

Report actual own-run computational measurements with the configuration and machine. Identify all simulated quantities as simulated. Keep proposed acquisition time and area coverage in a separate hypothetical budget. Include abstention, undetectable uniform absorption, contaminated-reference and focus-cancellation cases. Do not convert proxy variance to physical detection limits without calibration.

## Experience

Provide only the participant's actual relevant skills, prior projects and available capabilities. An AI assistant must not fabricate credentials or laboratory access.

## Risks

Discuss weak optical response, substrate variability, saturation, ambient flicker, reference provenance, persistent defects, geometrical changes, limited field of view, unseen surfaces, and insufficient differentiation from prior work. For each risk, state what evidence would reveal failure and what scope restriction or additional observation could address it.

## Development and collaboration

The participant can contribute reproducible inference software, interval analysis, experiment design and data analysis. A partner would be asked to provide representative professional surfaces, residue-ground-truth methods, available imaging equipment and field workflow validation. Treat costs and operating metrics as targets until measured or quoted.

## References

Use verified source URLs from docs/sources.md and any new sources read by the participant. Attach source-to-claim notes where useful. The main form should explain the proposal without requiring evaluators to run code.
