## Identity, deadline, and eligibility

- Challenge: **Novel Technologies for Rapid Proof of Clean in Professional Environments**.
- Seeker: Diversey, a Solenis company; platform: Innocentive.
- Public-page award: USD 10,000 for the best solution meeting requirements.
- Deadline: **2026-09-21 23:59 US Eastern Time**. On the current daylight-saving
  offset this is 2026-09-22 11:59 in China (UTC+8). Late submissions are not considered.
- Public eligibility exclusion: Solenis employees, their family members, and
  company affiliates cannot receive an award.
- Participation types shown by the public form: individual; organization;
  organization already owning the solution IP; or partnering/no-prize.
- Any additional nationality, residency, sanctions, age, organization, or team
  restrictions: **TO VERIFY in the logged-in Challenge Agreement**.

## Required submission format

The public page requires an English written proposal. The form requests:

1. participation type;
2. solution TRL;
3. partnering interest;
4. problem/opportunity and differentiation (up to 500 words);
5. solution overview against requirements (500 words, with summary/attachments available);
6. feasibility/scientific basis (up to 500 words);
7. performance expectations and limitations (up to 500 words);
8. relevant experience (up to 500 words);
9. solution risks and mitigations (up to 500 words);
10. development timeline, capability, deliverables, and desired role (up to 500 words);
11. online references (up to 500 words).

At most the final three submissions from one Solver are considered. Form content
is reviewed first; attachments are only supporting context, and attachment-only
submissions receive less attention.

## Solution boundary

### Must have

- credible method for verifying cleanliness;
- result within 30 minutes;
- suitability for professional cleaning environments;
- operation with basic training;
- no laboratory access or prohibitively expensive equipment;
- clean-versus-contaminated discrimination;
- clear scientific principles;
- significant differentiation from ATP testing;
- published evidence, technical rationale, prototype data, or a demonstrated foundation.

### Nice to have

- whole-room/large-area assessment;
- species- and preferably strain-level identification;
- portable/field-deployable and non-destructive operation;
- low consumable cost and minimal expertise;
- workflow integration, digital reporting, and audit trail.

### Explicitly out of scope

- laboratory turnaround beyond 30 minutes;
- ATP swab testing;
- conventional culture alone;
- standard fluorescence cleanliness tests;
- existing commercial monitoring without meaningful differentiation;
- hyperspectral imaging below TRL 6;
- PCR approaches below TRL 6.

TRL 2–4 concepts are invited generally. Commercially prominent emerging
approaches such as hyperspectral imaging and lab-on-chip PCR are only of
interest at TRL 6 or higher. The current project is Objective 1 only: optical
screening of in-scope surfaces. It does not satisfy species/strain identification.

## AI, IP, licensing, and post-award implications

- Public AI statement: submissions produced **solely** with generative AI are
  not of interest. The participant must make and document substantive research,
  source-review, experimental, scope, and authorship decisions. Exact disclosure
  wording or other AI conditions are **TO VERIFY** in the agreement.
- To receive an award, the Solver must grant Diversey non-exclusive license
  rights in the proposed-solution IP. The public page says there is no assignment.
- Rights in non-awarded proposals remain with the Solver.
- Diversey may explore collaboration where an organization already owns the IP,
  or where a Solver prefers partnership to a prize. Exact license field, term,
  territory, sublicensing, background-IP, improvement-IP, and diligence terms
  are **TO VERIFY**.
- Evaluation is theoretical against solution requirements. Possible post-award
  collaboration is described, but development, reporting, exclusivity, support,
  and delivery obligations are **TO VERIFY**.
- Confidentiality, publicity, export-control, payment onboarding, tax withholding,
  currency/fees, geographic payment availability, dispute law, and award timing:
  **TO VERIFY in the Terms of Use and Challenge Agreement**.

## Implication for this repository

The public page supports continued work on an early-TRL advanced optical concept,
but not a claim that the current synthetic proxy verifies professional cleanliness.
No submission should be made until a human participant reviews and accepts the
logged-in agreement, confirms eligibility/payment restrictions, and supplies the
substantive non-AI contribution required by the public guidance.
